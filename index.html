A PLACE BETWEEN PAST & PRESENT

A living monument.

An interactive interpretation of Stonehenge: the outer sarsen circle, a rising horseshoe of trilithons, smaller bluestones, and the surrounding earthwork.

Proportions follow English Heritage’s published measurements. The ruin arrangement is approximate, not an archaeological survey. Impact strength and fracture behavior are tuned for exploration.

Choose Launch, then click or tap a stone to throw a heavy sphere. Dragging always moves the camera. Try a shockwave in slow motion, then restore the stones.
